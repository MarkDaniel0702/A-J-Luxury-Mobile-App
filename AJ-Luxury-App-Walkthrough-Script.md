# A&J Luxury App Walkthrough: Taglish Script

Source: `AJ-Luxury-App-Walkthrough.pptx` (23 slides)

---

## Slide 1: App Walkthrough (Title)

[Show Slide 1: "App Walkthrough" title with the Home screen]

Hi everyone! Good day po sa inyong lahat.
Today, ipapakita namin ang A&J Luxury mobile app, step by step.
Dadaanan natin ang buying, ang selling at consigning, kung paano humingi ng help, at ang admin side.
Let's start!

---

## Slide 2: Step 1, Open the App

[Show the intro screens]

Pagbukas mo ng app, may ilang short intro screens muna.
Ine-explain dito ang buying, consigning, at condition grading.
I-tap mo lang ang Next para tumuloy.
Kung gusto mo nang dumiretso, i-tap ang Skip.
Diretso ka na sa login.

---

## Slide 3: Step 2, Login Page

[Show login page]

Ito naman ang login page.
Mag-login ka gamit ang email at password mo.
Pwede ka ring mag-create ng account dito.
Kung nakalimutan mo ang password, pwede mo itong i-reset.
Pwede ka ring mag-browse as a guest.
Para sa A&J staff, may hiwalay silang Admin login.

---

## Slide 4: Step 3, Log in or Continue as Guest

[Show the login and Continue as guest options]

So, dalawa ang choice mo dito.
Pwede kang mag-login para magamit lahat ng features.
O kaya, mag-continue as a guest.
As a guest, pwede kang mag-browse, mag-search, at magtanong sa chatbot.
Pero kailangan mo ng account para mag-save ng items.
Kailangan mo rin ng account para mag-inquire, mag-consign, at gumawa ng support ticket.

---

## Slide 5: Step 4, Home Screen

[Show Home screen]

Pag nakapasok ka na, ito ang Home screen.
Makikita mo dito ang newest arrivals.
Pwede ka ring mag-shop by category.
Sa baba, may menu.
Dito ka pupunta sa Shop, Consign, Help, Support, at sa Account mo.

---

## Slide 6: Step 5, Browse or Search Items

[Show Shop screen with search bar and filters]

Para maghanap ng item, pwede kang mag-search.
Pwede ka ring pumili ng category.
O kaya, i-filter mo by brand, condition, at price.
Sa bawat item, makikita mo ang brand, name, at price.

---

## Slide 7: Step 6, Item Detail Page

[Show item detail page]

Pag nag-tap ka ng item, mapupunta ka sa item detail page.
Pwede mong i-swipe ang mga photos.
I-check mo rin ang condition, brand, at price.
I-tap ang heart para i-save ang item.
I-tap ang Ask Help kung may tanong ka.
At kung gusto mo nang bilhin, i-tap ang Inquire.

---

## Slide 8: Step 7, Inquire to Buy

[Show Inquire to Buy form]

Ito ang inquiry form.
Ilalagay mo dito ang name at mobile number mo.
Pipili ka kung paano ka kokontakin ng A&J.
Pipili ka rin kung paano ka magbabayad, halimbawa, GCash.
Tapos, pili ka kung delivery o pick-up.
Pag okay na lahat, i-tap ang Send inquiry.

---

## Slide 9: Step 8, Inquiry Sent

[Show inquiry confirmation with reference number]

Pag na-send na, makakakuha ka ng reference number.
May summary din ng inquiry mo.
Next, magre-reply ang A&J.
Ico-confirm nila ang payment.
Tapos, aayusin nila ang delivery o pick-up.

---

## Slide 10: Step 9, Open the Consignment Section

[Show Consign tab]

Ngayon, punta naman tayo sa consignment.
Sa Consign tab, ine-explain kung paano gumagana ang consigning.
Nandito rin ang key terms.
Para magsimula, i-tap ang Start a consignment request.

---

## Slide 11: Step 10, Submit Item Details and Photos

[Show consignment form with photo upload]

Una, pipili ka kung sell o consign.
Tapos, ilagay mo ang brand, item name, at condition.
Mag-add ka rin ng photos.
Kailangan ang photo ng front, ng back, at ng serial o date code.

---

## Slide 12: Step 11, Review and Submit

[Show request summary and Submit request button]

Bago mag-submit, i-check mo muna ang summary ng request mo.
Pag okay na, i-tap ang Submit request.
Makakakuha ka agad ng reference number.

---

## Slide 13: Step 12, Track Consignment Status

[Show Account, then My consignments, with the status steps]

Para i-track ang item mo, punta ka sa Account.
Tapos, buksan mo ang My consignments.
Dito mo makikita kung nasaan na ang item mo.
Ito ang mga steps: Submitted, Received, Authenticated, Listed, Sold, tapos Paid out.

---

## Slide 14: Step 13, Open the Chatbot

[Show Help tab with the FAQ assistant]

Next, ang chatbot.
Sa Help tab, bubukas ang FAQ assistant.
May suggested questions na lalabas as buttons.
Kaya pwede ka na lang mag-tap.
Hindi mo na kailangang mag-type.

---

## Slide 15: Step 14, Ask or Choose a Question

[Show a typed question and the suggested question buttons]

Pwede kang mag-type ng sarili mong tanong.
Halimbawa, kung paano gumagana ang layaway.
O kaya, i-tap mo lang ang isa sa suggested questions.

---

## Slide 16: Step 15, Receive an Answer

[Show chatbot answer and the Talk to a support agent button]

Sasagot ang assistant gamit ang approved FAQs ng A&J.
Kung hindi ka niya matulungan, i-tap ang Talk to a support agent.
Magbubukas ito ng ticket.
Naka-attach na rin doon ang chat mo.

---

## Slide 17: Step 16, Open the Support Section

[Show Support tab]

Punta naman tayo sa Support tab.
Dito, pwede kang mag-open ng new ticket.
Pwede ka ring magtanong ng quick question sa Help assistant.
At pwede mong i-check ang existing tickets mo.

---

## Slide 18: Step 17, Submit a Support Ticket

[Show new support ticket form]

Para gumawa ng ticket, piliin mo muna kung tungkol saan ang problem.
Halimbawa, return o refund.
Piliin mo ang related order.
I-describe mo ang issue.
Tapos, i-tap ang Submit ticket.

---

## Slide 19: Step 18, Track Ticket Status

[Show ticket with reference number and status]

Makikita mo dito ang reference number ng ticket mo.
Makikita mo rin ang status: Open, In progress, o Resolved.
Balikan mo lang ito para basahin ang replies ng staff.
Pwede ka ring mag-reply dito.

---

## Slide 20: Step 19, Admin Login

[Show Admin login screen]

Ngayon, punta tayo sa admin side.
Ang A&J staff ay nagsa-sign in sa hiwalay na Admin login.
Ang customer at guest accounts ay hindi makakapasok sa admin screens.

---

## Slide 21: Step 20, Dashboard

[Show admin Dashboard]

Ito ang Dashboard.
Makikita dito ang sales, orders, new users, at consignments.
Makikita rin ang open tickets, reply speed, at chatbot activity.
Pwede mo itong tingnan for any period.
Ang mga new o unassigned tickets ay lalabas sa Needs attention.

---

## Slide 22: Step 21, Handle Support Tickets

[Show admin ticket view]

Para mag-handle ng ticket, i-open mo muna ito.
I-assign mo ito sa sarili mo.
I-update mo ang status, tapos mag-reply ka.
Makikita agad ng customer ang bawat update sa ticket nila.

---

## Slide 23: Step 22, Reports and Analytics

[Show Reports and Analytics screen]

Last, ang Reports and Analytics.
Pumili ka ng report type, date range, at category.
Makikita mo ang totals at ang chart.
Pwede mo ring i-download ang results as CSV o PDF.

---

## Closing

[Stay on Slide 23. The deck has no closing slide.]

Yan po ang A&J Luxury app, step by step.
Thank you po sa pakikinig!

- **Slide 10 (Step 9)** says the Consign tab shows the "key terms," but the deck doesn't list them.
- **Slide 15 (Step 14)** uses layaway as an example question, but the deck doesn't explain how layaway works.
- **Slide 8 (Step 7)** gives GCash only as an example. The deck doesn't list the other payment options.
- **New-arrival alerts** are not shown in the walkthrough, even though the `A&J LUXURY.pdf` presentation lists them as a feature.
