## Page 12: Buying and Selling in One App

[Show "Buying and Selling in One App" page with two columns]

Sa app, pwede kang bumili at magbenta sa iisang lugar.

**Buying muna.**
Find it fast. Pwede kang mag-search o mag-filter by brand, category, condition, at price.
See every detail. Pwede mong i-zoom ang photos para makita ang condition at kung may flaws.
Save your favorites. Makakakuha ka ng alerts pag may bagong pieces.
Ask to buy. Pipili ka kung paano magbabayad, like GCash, at kung delivery o pick-up.

**Selling and consigning naman.**
Sell. Bibilhin agad ng A&J ang piece mo.
Consign. Ang A&J ang magbebenta para sa'yo, tapos babayaran ka nila.
Send your item. I-fill in mo ang details at mag-add ng photos: front, back, at serial.
Track it. Masusundan mo ang bawat step, from Submitted to Paid out, gamit ang reference number mo.

## Page 15: Key Features

[Show "Key Features" page]

Ito ang key features ng app.

**FAQ Chatbot.**
Quick answers about buying, consigning, shipping, layaway, at returns, anytime.
Kung hindi niya kaya, ikokonekta ka niya sa staff member.

**Login and Guest Access.**
Pwede kang mag-browse kahit walang account.
Mag-sign in ka lang kung gusto mong mag-save ng items, mag-ask to buy, o mag-consign.

**Consignment Tracker.**
I-send mo ang item details step by step.
Tapos, i-track mo ang status from Submitted to Paid out.

**Support Tickets.**
Mag-report ng order o app problem.
Makakakuha ka ng reference number, status updates, at replies from staff.

**Zoomable Photos.**
I-swipe ang photos at i-zoom in para makita nang malapitan ang condition at flaws.

**Admin Reports.**
Makikita ng team n'yo ang sales, tickets, consignments, at ang most-asked questions.
Pwede ring i-download ang reports as CSV o PDF.

## Page 19: A&J Luxury App 12-Week Plan

[Show the 12-week Gantt chart]

Ito ang plan namin for 12 weeks, from planning hanggang launch.
Ang mga diamonds sa chart ay ang key checkpoints.

**Planning, Weeks 1 to 2.**
Kick-off at pag-agree sa features sa Week 1.
I-confirm namin ang fees, returns, at consignment rules ng A&J.
At ise-set up namin ang Google Play account.
Checkpoint: Features agreed.

**Design, Weeks 2 to 5.**
I-sketch namin ang screens at kung paano gagalaw ang users sa app.
Ide-design namin ang final look ng app at ang admin dashboard.
Sa Week 5, may quick test with 5 users.
Checkpoint: Design approved.

**Building the App, Weeks 2 to 10.**
Una ang database, logins, at photo storage.
Isusulat at ipa-approve namin ang chatbot answers.
Ikokonekta namin ang listings, inquiries, consignments, at tickets.
Gagawin din ang browse, search, item page, at saved items.
Sunod ang chatbot, support tickets, at alerts.
Pati na ang admin dashboard at reports.
At ang buying, o inquire to buy, at consigning.
Checkpoint: App features done, sa end ng Week 9.

**Testing, Weeks 6 to 12.**
Ite-test namin ang app sa iba't ibang phones.
Aayusin namin ang bugs at pabibilisin ang app.
May security check din.
At may trial run with A&J staff at customers.
Checkpoint: Ready for release, sa end ng Week 11.

**Launch, Weeks 4 to 12.**
Ihahanda namin ang privacy notice at terms of use.
Ipe-prepare at isu-submit namin ang Google Play listing.
At sa Week 12, ila-launch at iha-hand over namin ang app sa A&J.
Checkpoint: Launch and handover.
