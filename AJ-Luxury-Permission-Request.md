# A&J Luxury Mobile App — What to Request From A&J Luxury

**Prepared for:** project adviser · **Market:** Philippines · **Legend:** **[R]** Required · **[O]** Optional
**Adviser's instruction:** no pictures from the A&J Luxury website until A&J gives permission. Prototypes 2, 3 and 4 follow this: they contain no A&J photos, logo or testimonials.
**Legal note:** Philippine law references are from general knowledge and were not verified. Have counsel confirm before launch.

Where an A&J policy is quoted in the prototype (shipping, returns, consignment terms), it was paraphrased from A&J's public website text and is **not yet confirmed by A&J**. Items below marked ⚠ are known conflicts or gaps.

## Task 3 — Temporary replacement pictures

The same six drawings are styled three ways, one per prototype: soft tint with thin dark lines (Prototype 2), dark with gold lines and a diamond lattice (Prototype 3), and flat bold color blocks (Prototype 4). Each of the 24 sample listings uses one drawing and shows 4 or 5 views (front, back, detail, a flaw close-up when the item lists a flaw, and inclusions).

**If you later want real photos as placeholders:** Unsplash, Pexels and Pixabay offer free-to-use photos under their own licenses. Offline, the prototypes fall back to system fonts and still work.

## Task 4 — Prototypes 2, 3 and 4

**Open:** `prototype2/index.html`, `prototype3/index.html` or `prototype4/index.html` in a browser. No server or login needed. Keep the `shared/` folder next to them: all three use it for the phone frame, icons, placeholder pictures, sample catalogue and chatbot, so the FAQ answers live in one place (`shared/core.js`).

**Display:** each prototype shows inside a phone frame. The frame scales to the size of the browser window, so the whole phone is visible with no page scrolling. I checked window sizes from 1024×600 to 3840×1900, including 1920×957 and 1366×625 (a 1080p screen and a laptop screen with browser toolbars). **Monitor 1's resolution was left blank in the brief**, so the frame fits whatever the window is. On a window narrower than 520px the frame is dropped and the app fills the screen.

**All three have:** a short three-slide intro, then a login screen (any email and password works; sign-up and forgot-password screens included), a **guest, view-only** option, an FAQ chatbot, and navigation where every button leads to a working screen. Guests can browse, search, read FAQs and use the chatbot. Saving, inquiring, booking and consigning ask them to log in. The chatbot covers how to buy, consignment, selling for cash, payout, shipping, payment, layaway, authenticity, returns, condition ratings, the showroom, what guests can do, and "talk to a person". **It never says whether an item is authentic or fake.**

**Swiping (all three):** works by dragging with a mouse on a computer, or with a finger on a touchscreen.
- Photo galleries, the home carousel, the intro and the photo viewer move one picture at a time and snap into place. A quick flick, or a drag past a fifth of the width, turns the page.
- Rows of cards and chips scroll sideways. With a mouse they keep gliding briefly after you let go, like on a phone.
- Swipe the list left or right to change: the shop category (Prototypes 2 and 4), the collection tab All / Brand new / Pre-loved (Prototype 3), Home between Buy and Consign (Prototype 4), and the My activity tabs (Prototype 4).
- Swipe right from the left edge of the screen to go back. In Prototype 4's consign flow it goes back one question.
- Drag down to close bottom sheets, the photo viewer and Prototype 3's concierge chat. Swipe Prototype 3's menu to the left to close it.
- Pull down at the top of Home, shop and saved-item lists to refresh. It shows loading placeholders, then "Up to date". The sample data does not change.
- Photo viewer: double-tap or double-click to zoom, pinch on a touchscreen, or Ctrl + scroll wheel (or trackpad pinch) on a computer, then drag to look around.

**Also added:** loading placeholders when you first enter and when you refresh, tap feedback on cards and buttons, a heart "pop" when you save an item, smoother screen changes in Prototype 2, active-tab markers on the tab bars of Prototypes 2 and 4, and a Dark mode switch in Prototype 2 (Account > Display). The intro can be skipped, and "View intro" on the login screen shows it again.

| | Prototype 2 · Classic Boutique | Prototype 3 · Noir Salon | Prototype 4 · Bloom |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Look** | White and black, hairlines, light uppercase type | Near-black with champagne gold, serif display type, grain and diamond lattice | Cream, forest green, terracotta and butter, bold rounded type |
| **Layout** | Bottom tab bar (Home, Shop, Consign, Help, Account), 2-column grids | Slide-out menu, no tab bar, one large editorial card per row | Floating pill dock with a centre "+" button, bento tiles, bottom sheets |

| **Consign** | 4-step form (item, photos, details, hand-over) | One-page appraisal request | One question per screen with big tiles |
| **After you act** | Inquiry and consignment status pages | One "My requests" inbox for inquiries, viewings and appraisals | "My activity" with a progress tracker, plus notifications |
| **Own features** | Filter sheet (brand, condition, price), swipe gallery with zoom, searchable FAQs, profile settings, dark mode | Collections, brand directory, search, showroom **viewing booking** (day, time of day, pieces), authenticity guide, saved pieces | Buy / Consign switch, swipe-to-**Discover** cards, **layaway planner** and per-item estimate, price-slider filter, SMS-style sign-up code (any 4 digits), notifications |

**Sample data to know about:** items, brands, titles and prices are invented (24 listings). Prototype 4 shows one consignment labeled **SAMPLE** so the tracker has a mid-journey example. Layaway figures use the 30% down payment and equal monthly payments from A&J's site. No interest is added because the site gives no rate.

**Known limits:**
- No real accounts, no checkout and no uploads. Everything is simulated and resets when the page reloads. Photo slots add a drawn sample, not a real photo.
- No commission or fee amounts are shown, because A&J has not provided them. Policy text (shipping, returns, consignment terms) is paraphrased from A&J's public site and **not yet confirmed by A&J**. The shipping-fee conflict is flagged in the chatbot answer.
- Showroom address, hours, phone numbers and social accounts are placeholders marked "to be confirmed by A&J". Terms and Privacy pages are placeholders.
- The chatbot matches keywords against fixed answers. It is not an AI model and gives a fallback reply to questions outside the FAQ.
- Tested in Chromium only: automated checks of every screen and flow, plus every swipe tested with a mouse drag and with simulated touch. Not yet tried on Safari, Firefox or a real phone.
- Dark mode is in Prototype 2 only. Prototype 3 is already dark, and Prototype 4's bright colours are its identity.

## Task 5 — Technical Support and admin access (Prototypes 2, 3 and 4)

**Not done in Prototype 1.** Prototype 1 has no login, guest mode or chatbot to build on, and it uses A&J website photos, which the adviser's instruction does not allow. Support and admin were added to Prototypes 2, 3 and 4 only.

**Open:** same files as Task 4. To try both sides in one sitting, stay in the **same browser tab**:
1. Log in as a customer (any email and password) and open a support ticket.
2. Log out, tap **Admin login** on the log-in screen and sign in with the sample staff account **admin@ajluxury.test / Admin2026**. The new ticket is at the top of the dashboard under "Needs attention".
3. Reply, assign or change the status, log out, and log back in with the **same customer email**. The ticket shows "Updated", with the new status and the reply.

Everything still resets when the page reloads, and another tab does not see this tab's tickets.

**Technical Support (customers):**
- Reachable from the main navigation: a **Support** tab in Prototype 2's tab bar, **Support** in Prototype 3's menu, and a **Help** tab in Prototype 4's dock (it was "Ava") with an "Ask Ava | Support tickets" switch. Also linked from the account or Me page, the footer, Prototype 4's "+" button, and the chatbot.
- **New ticket:** choose the type (order issue, return or refund request, authenticity concern, app problem), look up the order or consignment it is about, then add a subject and description. Each customer gets five sample past orders and consignments to choose from, plus any inquiry, consignment, viewing or layaway request they made in the session. A confirmation screen shows the ticket reference.
- **My tickets:** a list with status filters (All, Open, In progress, Resolved). Swipe the list left or right to change the filter. Each customer starts with one resolved ticket labelled **Sample**.
- **Ticket page:** status timeline, the linked order or consignment, who is handling it, the full message history, and a reply box. Replying to a resolved ticket reopens it.
- **Chatbot escalation:** when the customer asks for a person, or the chatbot does not understand, the answer shows **Talk to a support agent**. It opens a new ticket with the question as the subject and attaches the recent chat. A new FAQ answer covers "help with an order or consignment".
- Guests can see Support and use the chatbot, but must log in to open or view tickets (the same rule as other account actions).

**Admin (staff only):**
- Separate **Admin login** screen with its own staff account. Customer and guest sessions that open an admin address see a "Staff only" page. Logged-out visits go to Admin login. The staff email is refused on the customer log-in form. Once signed in, staff stay in the admin area.
- **Dashboard:** period switch (7 days, 30 days, 90 days, 12 months); tiles for sales, orders, new users, consignments (each with the change against the previous period), open tickets, average resolution time, chatbot questions and escalations; "Needs attention" list (new or unassigned tickets); charts for sales over time, sales by category, tickets opened, average resolution time by ticket type, chatbot questions over time and top chatbot topics. Tap or hover a bar to read its value.
- **Tickets:** queues (Active, Unassigned, Mine, Open, In progress, Resolved, All) and search by ticket, customer or order number. A ticket shows the customer, the linked order or consignment, the attached chat, and the message history. Staff can change the status, assign it (or "Assign to me"), reply, and close or reopen it. Every change is logged in the history the customer sees.
- **Reports:** sales, consignments, support tickets or users, filtered by date range (or Last 7 days to 12 months) and by category (item category, ticket type or user type). Shows totals, a chart and the first 50 rows. **CSV** downloads every row and column. **PDF** opens the browser's print dialog with a clean report page: choose "Save as PDF".
- **Team:** each sample agent's active and recently resolved tickets, and log out.
- Prototype 4's admin keeps its "+" button for quick actions (unassigned tickets, my tickets, sales report, ticket report).

**Sample data:** about a year of orders, consignment requests, sign-ups, support tickets and chatbot questions, generated in `shared/core.js` with a fixed seed so the numbers are the same on every load. Dates count back from today, so recent periods always have data. Names are invented and emails use `example.com`. Brands and items are the invented catalogue from Task 4. Tickets submitted and chatbot questions asked in the session are added to the dashboard and reports.

**Known limits:**
- Access control and data live in the browser only. A real app must check staff access on the server, use A&J's own staff accounts and roles, and store tickets in a database.
- One sample staff account (Bea Santos, support lead). The other three agents can be assigned but cannot log in.
- No email, SMS or push notifications are sent. Prototype 4 adds an in-app notification when a ticket is submitted or updated.
- Response times, refund rules and escalation hours are not shown, because A&J has not provided them. Ticket text says response times are still to be confirmed by A&J.
- PDF export uses the browser's "Save as PDF" rather than a generated file.
- Tested in Chromium only, at 1920×957 and at phone width: automated checks of every new flow (customer, guest, admin), the status round trip, the CSV contents, the print layout, and a regression run of the existing flows. Not yet tried on Safari, Firefox or a real phone.
